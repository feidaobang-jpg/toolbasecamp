平安县城，开炮！我在虫潮围城把亮剑名场面做成了能玩的战役

【简介（中文主版）】
试玩入口（首行）：https://www.zhengxiaohui.cn/games.html —— 在列表选择「虫潮围城」，主菜单进入「抗战战役 · 亮剑名场面」。
这期给虫潮围城加了一个独立剧情模式：抗战战役，共三章，把亮剑里几个名场面改编成能真打的关卡。
第一章李家坡：沿交通壕推进，手雷炸掉两座机枪堡垒，再夺坡顶指挥阵地。
第二章骑兵连：上马冲刺挥刀冲破封锁线，再护送运输车抵达撤离点。
第三章平安县城：搬炮弹、接管攻城炮，落点环对准城门连开三炮轰开城门，带队进城打巷战。
全程正常规则通关，未开无敌、未改数值；三章可随时重玩、检查点续关。
抗战战役这一版用 Codex 开发。
本期提供中英可切换字幕。

【标题（英文翻译字段）】
Fire on Pingan County! I turned famous scenes into a playable campaign in Swarm Siege

【简介（英文翻译字段）】
Play it here (first line): https://www.zhengxiaohui.cn/games.html — pick "Swarm Siege" from the list, then enter "Anti-Japanese War Campaign" from the main menu.
This update adds a standalone story mode to Swarm Siege: a three-chapter campaign that turns famous wartime scenes into real, playable battles.
Chapter 1 Lijia Slope: push along the trenches, grenade two machine-gun bunkers, then take the hilltop command post.
Chapter 2 Cavalry: mount up, charge and saber through the blockade line, then escort the supply wagon to the evac point.
Chapter 3 Pingan County: haul shells, man the siege cannon, line the impact ring on the gate and blow it open in three shots, then fight street by street into town.
Completed on normal rules — no invincibility, no stat edits; all three chapters are replayable with checkpoint resume.
This campaign version was developed with Codex.
Switchable Chinese / English captions included.
