English description
Play my games: https://www.zhengxiaohui.cn/games.html
Pick "虫潮围城" (Swarm Siege) in the list and choose "虫族模式" (Swarm Mode) on the main menu; the game UI is currently Chinese only.
A Bilibili player ("我不是abbie") asked in the comments: can you make a mode where I am the swarm? It shipped the same day - you no longer defend the base, you ARE the swarm.
The rules: destroy the human base core within 360 seconds. WASD drives the Mother, J bites, K spits acid, U summons warriors, I summons flyers, O molts to heal, L dashes. Every point of damage you deal comes back as Biomass, which pays for summons and healing. The Mother can die at most 5 times; on the fifth the swarm collapses.
This gameplay was recorded under normal rules on the live web build - no stat edits, no speed changes. We did grind the 2500-HP gate down to zero, but the 2000-HP core behind it never lost a single point of HP. This mode is much harder than I expected, so it is a genuine challenge mode now. If you can crack the core, come prove me wrong.
Swarm Mode was developed by Qoder; this video (capture, narration, edit) was also produced by Qoder. It is playable on the website today.
