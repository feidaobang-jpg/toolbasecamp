Breachline adaptation (2026): normalize to metre scale and a 3.6m height,
apply the original mirror, normalize skin influences, bevel hard edges,
convert the original camo3 and normal maps to a contemporary material,
export the original rig and adapted mesh to GLB. Source legacy clips were
not loaded by Blender 5.2; runtime ground-contact and aiming motion is a
new adaptation rather than the supplier's animation clips.

The adapted model, textures, and included mech_view.gd/walker_feet.gd
runtime animation adaptation are also available under CC BY-SA 3.0.
Reusable model and editable Blender source are supplied in the player's
third-party-assets/mech folder. No endorsement by Skorpio is implied.
Other game assets retain their separately stated licenses.
